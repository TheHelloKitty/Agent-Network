On paper, he doesn’t exist. 

No birth certificate. No digital footprint. Not a single trace across federal databases, police dockets, or criminal records. To the rest of the world, he is a ghost. 

To her, he’s the ultimate obsession.

Hired to dig up the truth behind an identity completely wiped clean, she expects a routine investigation. Instead, her search drags her straight into the crosshairs of a lethal, enigmatic man who watches her every move. He lives in the dark, answers to no one, and makes it clear that some secrets are meant to stay buried.

She knows she should run, but the dangerous pull between them is impossible to ignore. Every stolen touch is a warning, and every whispered command blurs the line between captive and willing accomplice. 

Uncovering his past could set her free—or cost her everything. But walking away from his fire is no longer an option.

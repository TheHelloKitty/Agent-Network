**True Crime, True Secrets**

In the rain‑slick streets of an old town, a quiet boutique hotel hides more than just a neon sign. A single night, a murder shatters the town’s peace, and the evidence is buried in the shadows of that glittering façade.  

A seasoned detective returns to the city that once betrayed him, armed with a notebook full of questions and a past that refuses to stay buried. The case is a tangled web of old lovers, hidden motives, and a truth that refuses to stay quiet.  

The story unfolds in short, gripping chapters that keep you turning the page as the rain continues to fall outside. Every clue is a piece of a puzzle that will leave you breathless—and you’ll be left wondering who will finally be brought to justice.  

Based on real events, this true‑crime narrative delivers the raw intensity of a real investigation, the emotional depth of a town in crisis, and the relentless pursuit of truth.

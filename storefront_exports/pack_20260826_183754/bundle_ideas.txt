### How to Set It Up on Payhip

1. **Create the Digital Products**  
   - Upload the PDF, ePub, MP3, MP4, JPEG, and any other files as separate “files” under the product.  
   - For the Live Q&A, upload a placeholder “Zoom Link” that you’ll update before the session.

2. **Bundle Construction**  
   - In Payhip, use the “Bundle” feature (or create a product with multiple downloadable files).  
   - Label each bundle clearly (e.g., “The Full Case Bundle”) and write a short, benefit‑focused description.

3. **Pricing Strategy**  
   - The base eBook can be sold at a lower price (e.g., $4.99) to capture impulse buyers.  
   - Bundles should be priced at a “sweet spot”—high enough to cover added content, low enough that buyers feel they’re getting a discount versus buying each component separately.

4. **Upsell & Cross‑Sell**  
   - After a purchase, show a “Would you like to upgrade to the next bundle?” pop‑up.  
   - Offer a small discount if they buy a bundle within 48 h of the base eBook purchase.

5. **Marketing Tips**  
   - Use the 5‑chapter teaser you already have as a lead magnet.  
   - Post on true‑crime forums, Reddit, Discord servers, and Facebook groups.  
   - Highlight the exclusive audio/video content in your ads—people love “behind‑the‑scenes” material.  
   - Run a limited‑time “Early‑Bird” discount for the first 50 buyers of each bundle.

### Final Thought
By offering a mix of audio, visual, and interactive content, you turn a single story into a multi‑layered experience that appeals to different learning styles and price sensitivities. The bundles above are designed to keep the perceived value high while making the purchase process simple and enticing for true‑crime fans on Payhip. Happy selling!

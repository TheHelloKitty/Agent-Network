## Quick Implementation Checklist (Payhip‑Ready)

1. **Create the digital files**  
   - PDF/ePub/MOBI for the ebook.  
   - MP3/AAC for audio chapters.  
   - High‑resolution PNG/PDF for posters, wallpapers, cover art.  
   - Word/Google Docs templates for character sheets & journal.  

2. **Set up product pages**  
   - Use a **clear, compelling title** (e.g., “Emma & Lucas: A Rain‑Soaked Reunion – Starter Pack”).  
   - Add a **short video teaser** (30 s reading a romantic line with background music).  
   - List **key benefits** in bullet form (what the buyer gets immediately).  

3. **Pricing & Discounts**  
   - Enable **Payhip’s “Discount Codes”** for early‑bird (e.g., “LAUNCH10” for 10 % off).  
   - Set up a **“Buy One, Get One 50 % off”** for the Reader’s Deluxe + Audiobook add‑on.  

4. **Delivery & Automation**  
   - Upload all files to Payhip’s **Digital Delivery** system.  
   - For the **Webinar** (Bundle 4), create a private YouTube/Zoom link and embed the URL in the purchase confirmation email.  

5. **Marketing Assets**  
   - **Email sequence** (3‑email launch series).  
   - **Social‑media graphics** (use the same poster/wallpaper designs).  
   - **Pinterest pins** – romance readers love “book‑aesthetic” pins.  

6. **Collect Feedback**  
   - After 2 weeks, send a **quick survey** (Google Form) to purchasers asking: “What extra content would you love to see next?” Use the answers to iterate future bundles (e.g., a sequel bundle, or a “Romance Writing Challenge”).

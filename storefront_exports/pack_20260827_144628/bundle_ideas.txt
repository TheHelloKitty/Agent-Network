### Quick Tips for Launching on Payhip

1. **Create a compelling landing page** – Use the opening paragraph you already have (the rain‑soaked lounge scene) as the hero copy. Pair it with a mood‑board image (rain, neon, vintage hotel) to set the tone.
2. **Offer a “Free Sample”** – Upload the first 2‑3 pages as a downloadable PDF. Payhip lets you collect email addresses for future launches.
3. **Leverage Payhip’s “Upsell” feature** – After a buyer purchases the basic e‑book, automatically suggest the Audio + e‑Book bundle as a one‑click add‑on.
4. **Run a launch countdown** – Set a “pre‑order” period (e.g., 7 days) where the basic e‑book is $4.99, then unlock the full bundle at the suggested price on release day.
5. **Collect reviews early** – Send the finished manuscript to 10–15 romance‑bloggers or BookTok creators in exchange for an honest review. Positive social proof drives higher conversion on Payhip’s product page.

With these bundles, you’ll be able to monetize the same core story in **multiple formats and price points**, turning a single romance manuscript into a small, sustainable indie publishing business. Good luck, and may the rain‑kissed streets of your story bring plenty of sales!

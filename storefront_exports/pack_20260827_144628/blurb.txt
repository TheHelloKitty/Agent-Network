**Lost in the Rain, Found in the Heart**

When a storm turns a sleepy town into a slick, silver mirror, two old lovers find themselves drawn together in the dim glow of a boutique hotel. Emma, now a successful city gallery curator, returns to the place where she first fell in love, hoping the past will stay buried. But the rain brings memories—and Lucas—back to the old square, and the quiet streets hold more than just echoes of their laughter.

Their reunion is electric and tender, a delicate dance between what was and what could be. A single glance, a hesitant laugh, and a shared secret of a childhood bench ignite a longing that neither time nor distance has fully erased. Will they let the past guide them into a new future, or will the shadows of old wounds keep them apart?

This is a story of second chances, of love that refuses to fade, and of the courage it takes to open your heart again. Every page is a promise that, even after the hardest storms, there is still light to be found.

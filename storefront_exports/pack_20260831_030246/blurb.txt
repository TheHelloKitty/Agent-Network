Five years of silence couldn’t extinguish the fire between them. One rainy night is about to ignite it all over again.

Emma thought she had finally moved on. Leaving her hometown and pouring her soul into her art was supposed to heal the ache Lucas left behind when he walked out of her life. She convinced herself she was safe from the past.

Until a chance encounter brings her face-to-face with the only man who ever truly owned her heart. 

Lucas is back, sharper and more dangerously magnetic than she remembers. The regret in his eyes is matched only by a raw, breathless hunger that makes Emma’s hard-won defenses crumble in an instant. 

He claims he wants to rewrite their story. But behind the apologies is an undeniable pull—a scorching, suffocating heat that proves the years apart only made their desire burn hotter. 

Letting him in once shattered her world. Giving in to him now might consume her completely.

### How to Set It Up on Payhip

1. **Create a product for the core eBook** – keep the base price low (e.g., $9.99) so customers see value in upgrading.
2. **Add “bundles”** as separate products. In the product description, list each included item and highlight the added value (audio, printable, video, etc.).
3. **Use Payhip’s “Add-ons” feature** if you want customers to be able to add extra items to the base eBook at checkout (e.g., “Add the audiobook for $15”).
4. **Provide preview links** (e.g., a short audio clip, a sample page of the evidence log) so buyers feel confident.
5. **Offer a limited‑time discount** (e.g., 10% off the Investigator’s Toolkit during launch week) to drive early sales.

### Quick Tips for Maximizing Sales

- **Tagging & SEO**: Use relevant tags like `true crime`, `mystery`, `crime fiction`, `investigation`, `audio book`, etc.
- **Email Upsell**: After a purchase, send a thank‑you email offering the next bundle at a small discount.
- **Social Proof**: Add a brief testimonial or quote from a satisfied reader in each bundle description.
- **Bundle Size**: Keep each bundle small enough that buyers feel they’re getting a “complete” package, but large enough to justify the price.

Feel free to tweak the bundle contents and prices to match your audience’s preferences and your production costs. Happy selling on Payhip!

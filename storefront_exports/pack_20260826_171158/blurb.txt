**Rain‑kissed streets, hidden secrets, and a truth that refuses to stay buried.**  
In a town where neon lights flicker over rain‑slick cobblestones, a shocking discovery turns the quiet nights into a maze of questions.  

Emma, a former local who left to escape the town’s shadows, is drawn back when an old friend’s voice echoes through the boutique hotel’s lounge. Lucas, the man who once held her heart, now carries a past that is as tangled as the city’s night‑time drizzle.  

Together, they peel back layers of a crime that still haunts the town’s memories—one that was never solved, one that still lingers in the scent of sandalwood and the echo of a jazz piano. Their past, their present, and the truth collide, forcing them to confront what was left behind and what is still hidden beneath the surface.  

If you’re drawn to true crime stories that blend atmosphere, emotion, and relentless curiosity, this book invites you to follow a trail of clues, old scars, and a town that refuses to stay silent.  

**Discover the truth that’s been waiting for someone to listen.**

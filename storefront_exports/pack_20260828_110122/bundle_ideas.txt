### How to Set Up Each Bundle on Payhip

1. **Create a Product Page**  
   - Title it with the bundle name.  
   - Add a brief, enticing description (use the “Why It Works” column as a guide).  
   - Upload the files in the correct format (PDF, MP3, etc.).  

2. **Price and Discounts**  
   - Set the base price as suggested.  
   - Offer a 10–15 % discount on the first 50 copies to create urgency.  
   - Use Payhip’s “Add to Cart” button for instant checkout.  

3. **Marketing Hooks**  
   - Highlight the “exclusive” items (e.g., behind‑the‑scenes PDF, art prints).  
   - Mention that the audiobook is narrated by a professional voice actor.  
   - Use the “Author’s Notes” as a teaser for future books in the series.  

4. **Post‑Purchase Upsell**  
   - After purchase, offer a 10 % discount on your next book or a separate “Future Chapter Teaser” PDF.  
   - Encourage sharing on social media with a custom hashtag (e.g., #RainyStreetLove).

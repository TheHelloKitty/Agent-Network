**Blurb**  
The rain had turned the streets of the old town into a glossy river of reflections, the neon signs of the boutique hotel across the square blinking in half‑light like hesitant eyes. Inside the hotel’s lounge, the amber glow of a lone chandelier threw soft halos over deep‑green leather chairs and a mahogany bar that stretched along the far wall. The scent of sandalwood mingled with fresh coffee, and somewhere in the back a slow jazz piano drifted, its notes curling like smoke around the patrons.

Emma lingered at the edge of the room, her fingertips brushing a marble table, trying to smooth out the tremor rising in her chest. She’d left town years ago for the city, a job at the gallery, and a life that felt both new and hollow. But when Lucas stepped into the lounge, his familiar silhouette against the rain, the world narrowed to the space between them. Their eyes met, memories flickered—old scars, shared secrets, the bench in the park, the laughter that once filled the air.

He apologized for leaving, for the fear that had kept him away. She listened, heart pounding, wondering if the past could be rewritten. Together, they confront the ghosts of what was, and the possibility of what could be. “Rekindled” is a tender, atmospheric tale that shows love is never truly lost, only waiting for the right moment to be reborn.

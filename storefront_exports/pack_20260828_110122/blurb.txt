**Untitled**

After five years of city lights and silent canvases, Emma returns to the rain‑kissed streets of her hometown, hoping to leave the past behind. When the familiar glow of the boutique hotel’s lounge catches her eye, she sees Lucas—taller, weathered, and still wearing the same charcoal coat that once wrapped their shared secrets. Their eyes lock, and the world seems to pause on a single breath.

Will the old flame that once burned bright find new fire, or will the storm that kept them apart finally break? In this tender, atmospheric romance, every whispered apology and lingering glance is a promise of something that feels both impossible and inevitable.

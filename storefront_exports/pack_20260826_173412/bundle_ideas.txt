### How to Build & Sell These Bundles on Payhip

1. **Package everything in a single ZIP file**  
   - Inside: PDF, EPUB, MP3(s), video file (if applicable), and any supplementary PDFs.  
   - Name the ZIP after the bundle (e.g., `TrueCrime_Collector_Edition.zip`) to avoid confusion.

2. **Set up the product on Payhip**  
   - Create a new product for each bundle.  
   - Upload the ZIP, add a compelling description that highlights the extra content.  
   - Use the “Bundling” feature (or just create separate products and let customers choose) – Payhip automatically handles the download links.

3. **Promote the bundles**  
   - Highlight the added value in your marketing copy: “Unlock the full mystery—bonus chapters, an exclusive interview, and a printable case file!”  
   - Offer a **limited‑time discount** on the first bundle to kick‑start sales (e.g., 20% off for the first week).

4. **Collect email addresses**  
   - For the Ultimate Fan Bundle, ask buyers to provide an email for the monthly newsletter.  
   - Use a simple form or Payhip’s “Buyers” export to start building a mailing list.

5. **Keep content fresh**  
   - For the monthly newsletter bundle, schedule a 2‑3 week turnaround so you can keep the community engaged without overwhelming yourself.

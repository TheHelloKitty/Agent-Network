**Untitled**  
*True Crime*

In the rain‑slick streets of a quiet town, an old secret refuses to stay buried. When a quiet night turns into a desperate hunt, the residents are forced to confront the shadows of their past.  

The story follows Emma, a once‑promising artist who left town to escape a painful history, and Lucas, a man whose return stirs memories that have been kept alive in hushed whispers for years. Their reunion is a delicate dance of guilt, longing, and the promise of redemption.  

As the city’s underbelly begins to surface, the line between victim and perpetrator blurs. Every clue points to a truth that could shatter the town’s fragile peace.  

If you’re drawn to stories where the past refuses to stay buried and the truth is as elusive as a midnight rain, this book will keep you turning pages.  

Discover what lies beneath the surface—when you open the cover.

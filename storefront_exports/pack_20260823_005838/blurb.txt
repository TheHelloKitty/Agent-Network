**Untitled**

Rain slicks the cobblestones, neon flickers, and the old town’s secrets hum in the background.  
Emma has left the town that shaped her, chasing a new life in the city—until the day she finds Lucas back where they once shared laughter and stolen kisses.  

Their eyes lock across a dimly lit hotel lounge, and old memories stir like a forgotten melody. Will the past be enough to rewrite their future, or will the distance between them stay forever?  

Feel the ache of longing, the warmth of a second chance, and the quiet hope that love can bloom again, even when the world feels like a glossy river of reflections.  

**Dive into this tender, rain‑kissed reunion and let your heart follow Emma and Lucas back to where it all began.**

### Quick Tips for Launching on Payhip

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **1️⃣ Create a compelling sales page** – Use the opening paragraph you already have as a hook, add a few “What you’ll get” bullet points, and insert a mock‑up of the cover + a screenshot of a bookmark. |
| **2️⃣ Offer a “Free Sample”** – Let visitors download Chapter 1 (or the audio snippet) for free in exchange for an email. Use Payhip’s built‑in email capture to nurture leads. |
| **3️⃣ Bundle discounts** – Enable Payhip’s “Buy X, get Y% off” rule. For example, if a buyer adds Bundle 1 + Bundle 3 to the cart, automatically apply a 10 % discount. |
| **4️⃣ Upsell after purchase** – After the checkout, redirect buyers to a “Thank‑you” page that promotes the higher‑priced bundles (e.g., “You loved Chapter 1? Grab the full Writer’s Blueprint for 30 % off”). |
| **5️⃣ Leverage social proof** – Once you have a few sales, request short testimonials and display them prominently. Use the “Meet the Lovers” PDF as a shareable social‑media teaser. |

### How the Bundles Add Value

| Bundle | Why It’s Attractive to Buyers |
|--------|------------------------------|
| Starter | Perfect for readers who just want the story and a quick visual cheat‑sheet—low entry cost, high perceived value. |
| Narrative Immersion | Audio makes the book feel like a podcast episode, ideal for commuters or audiobook lovers; diary gives a peek behind the curtain. |
| Collector’s | Tangible book + signed items + a mini‑documentary satisfy collectors and fans who love physical media. |
| Masterclass | Adds educational content; appeals to true‑crime hobbyists who want to learn investigative techniques. |
| Ultimate | Combines everything + a personal interaction; a premium experience for the most dedicated fans and collectors. |

### Tips for Selling on Payhip

1. **Use Tiered Pricing** – let buyers “upgrade” as they go, so they can start with the Starter Bundle and add more later.
2. **Offer a Limited‑Time Discount** – e.g., 10 % off the Collector’s Edition for the first 48 hrs to create urgency.
3. **Bundle Bundles** – let customers mix and match (e.g., add the audio to the Starter Bundle for an extra $3.00).
4. **Highlight Author Credentials** – add a short bio and mention any prior true‑crime works to build trust.
5. **Provide a Preview** – a 3‑minute audio clip or a sample of the character cheat‑sheet keeps buyers engaged.

Feel free to tweak the contents or prices to match your target audience’s preferences and your production costs. Happy selling!

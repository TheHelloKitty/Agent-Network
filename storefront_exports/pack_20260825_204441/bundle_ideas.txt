### Quick How‑To‑Set‑Up Each Bundle on Payhip  

1. **Create a Product** → “Digital product” → upload the primary file (PDF for the ebook).  
2. **Add “Add‑ons”** (Payhip lets you bundle multiple files under one product). Upload the extra PDFs, MP3, and MP4 files for the higher‑tier bundles.  
3. **Set the Price** (use the numbers above).  
4. **Write a Compelling Description** – copy the “Why It Sells” bullet points above, add a short teaser from Chapter 1, and include a *“What you’ll get”* checklist (e.g., “✔️ Full draft PDF, ✔️ Research notes, ✔️ Audio narration…”).  
5. **Enable “Instant download”** and turn on “Allow customers to download updates” for the Deluxe bundle (this is the feature that delivers future revisions automatically).  
6. **Add a Cover Image** – even a simple “Untitled – True Crime Draft” mock‑up works; later you can replace it with the final cover once it’s designed.

### Bonus Tips for Maximising Sales  

| Tip | How to Execute |
|-----|----------------|
| **Launch a “pre‑order” for the final version** | Offer the $4.99 Starter Pack now, promising a free upgrade to the final, polished ebook once it’s finished. Payhip automatically sends the new file to existing buyers. |
| **Cross‑promote on true‑crime forums & subreddits** | Share a short excerpt (the opening scene you posted) and a link to the $4.99 starter. Include a “Ask Me Anything” thread where you reveal a hidden clue from Chapter 7 – drives traffic and credibility. |
| **Email capture** | Add a simple opt‑in form (Mailchimp, ConvertKit) on the product page offering a **free “True‑Crime Checklist” PDF**. Use the list to announce bundle upgrades later. |
| **Limited‑time “bundle discount”** | Run a 48‑hour flash sale on the Deluxe bundle (e.g., $34.99) to create urgency. Payhip’s “Coupon” feature makes this a one‑click setup. |
| **Upsell at checkout** | When someone buys the $4.99 starter, show a one‑click “Add the Research Bundle for $5 more?” – Payhip’s “Add‑on” option works like a mini‑checkout upsell. |

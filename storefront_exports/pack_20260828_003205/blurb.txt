In a rain‑kissed town where neon lights flicker like secret glances, Emma returns to the place she thought she’d left behind forever. She’s built a life of quiet galleries and guarded hearts, but the moment the door swings open, a familiar silhouette steps inside—Lucas, the love she never stopped dreaming about, now older, scarred, and undeniably drawn back to her.

Their reunion is a dance of hesitant smiles and lingering memories, set against the warm glow of a vintage lounge where jazz piano whispers promises of unfinished chapters. As old wounds surface and new hopes ignite, Emma and Lucas must decide whether the past is a locked door or a doorway to a second chance.

With lush, atmospheric prose and a chemistry that crackles like rain on cobblestones, this novel captures the tender tug of “what if” and the fierce pull of destiny.

Ready to feel the ache of longing and the thrill of love rediscovered? Open the cover and let Emma and Lucas’s story sweep you into a world where every glance could rewrite a lifetime.

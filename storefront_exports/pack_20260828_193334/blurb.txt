**Untitled**  
*When a storm brings them back together, two hearts must decide whether to let the past stay buried or to rewrite their future.*

After years away, Emma returns to the rain‑slick streets of her hometown, chasing a new life in the city. The boutique hotel’s glow is a reminder of the night she first met Lucas, and the scent of sandalwood carries the memory of a love that felt like home.

When their eyes finally lock again, the quiet hum of the jazz piano seems to echo the words left unsaid. Old wounds surface, but so do the quiet hopes that have lingered in the corners of their minds. Will they let the past close the door, or will they risk the possibility of a second chance?

*Discover the fragile balance between memory and longing, and the quiet courage it takes to open a heart again.*

Take a step into their story—because sometimes the most beautiful love stories are the ones that finally find their way back home.

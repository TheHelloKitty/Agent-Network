**Untitled**

Rain‑slick streets, a boutique hotel, and a reunion that turns into a confession.  
In a town where the neon glow hides more than it reveals, Emma and Lucas find each other again—only to unearth a secret that has been buried for years.

What began as a chance encounter becomes a race against time. A missing person, a hidden ledger, and a crime that the town has tried to forget.  
Every clue leads deeper into a web of betrayal and unanswered questions, and the truth threatens to shatter the fragile peace of the community.

Follow the trail of whispered motives and hidden motives. Witness how a past once thought forgotten can ignite a fire that burns through the present.  
This is a true‑crime story that will keep you turning pages long after the final chapter.

**Discover the truth. Let the story set you free.**

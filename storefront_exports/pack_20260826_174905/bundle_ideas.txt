### How to Set It Up on Payhip

1. **Create a product listing for the base eBook** (PDF & EPUB).  
2. **Add each bundle as a separate product** or use Payhip’s “Bundled Products” feature to let customers pick the bundle they want.  
3. **Upload all files** (audio, PDFs, video, images) to the bundle’s product page.  
4. **Set the suggested price** above (or adjust slightly based on your audience research).  
5. **Use Payhip’s discount codes** to run occasional promotions (e.g., “STARTER10” for the Starter Sleuth Pack).

### Quick Tips

- **Keep file sizes small** (compress audio and images) so checkout stays fast.  
- **Include a clear “What’s Inside” section** for each bundle so buyers know exactly what they’re getting.  
- **Add a teaser** (e.g., a short audio clip or a preview of the bonus chapter) to entice buyers.  
- **Promote the bundles on social media** with short videos or screenshots of the printable worksheets and art prints.

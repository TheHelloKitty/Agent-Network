**Title:** *When Rain Falls*

**Blurb**

In the rain‑kissed streets of a sleepy town, a forgotten love is suddenly pulled back into the light. Emma, now a thriving city artist, returns to the old boutique hotel where her heart once beat for a man named Lucas. Their eyes lock across a jazz‑filled lounge, and the past refuses to stay buried in silence.

A decade of distance, city lights, and quiet regrets has left both of them with scars that only the right touch can heal. As they walk the familiar cobblestones again, old memories stir—soft whispers of shared laughter, stolen kisses, and the promise that once seemed impossible. But will their new beginnings be bright enough to outshine the shadows of what they left behind?

Dive into a story that blends the scent of rain, the echo of a piano, and the tender ache of a second chance. Feel the pull of destiny, the warmth of a reunion, and the hope that love, no matter how old, can still bloom anew.

*Discover their story, and let your heart follow where it has always wanted to go.*

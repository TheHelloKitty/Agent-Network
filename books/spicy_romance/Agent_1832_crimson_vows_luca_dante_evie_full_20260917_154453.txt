# Crimson Vows

### Blurb

To the blood-soaked streets of Chicago’s underworld, he is Dante—an unyielding enforcer whose mercy was buried long ago. To his syndicate family, he is Luca Borelli, the loyal son burdened with preserving an empire built on bone and bullet casings. He keeps his lethal dual existence strictly divided, until the night he walks into The Velvet Lantern.

Evie Caruso knows the dangers of the shadows better than anyone. Running a dimly lit, velvet-draped cocktail lounge on contested territory requires nerve, poise, and absolute neutrality. But when a ruthless rival family targets her bar to settle an old score left by her late father, Evie finds herself backed into a corner with only one impossible exit: a blood oath with the city’s most feared predator.

Luca offers her untouchable protection under one condition—she must wear his ring and seal their alliance in blood. Trapped under his roof and bound by vows stained crimson, Evie expects a cold-blooded monster. Instead, she finds a man who burns with a dark, consuming hunger meant only for her.

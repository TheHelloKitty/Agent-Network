# CHAPTER 1
### The Last Pint

The Velvet Lantern kept its own weather.

Rain battered the alleyway outside, but inside, the air hung thick and warm—sour-sweet with spilled rye, lemon peel, and decades of polished timber. The neon sign over the front glass bled through the condensation in a raw pink smear. Evie Caruso wiped the same ring of mahogany until the grain gleamed under the amber pendant lights. If she stopped moving, she would have to listen to the three men at the front table breathing like they owned the floorboards beneath their boots.

They didn’t. 

Not yet.

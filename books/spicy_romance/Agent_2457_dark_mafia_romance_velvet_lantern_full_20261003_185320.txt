# Crimson Vows

### Blurb
In the smoke-choked shadows of The Velvet Lantern, loyalty is paid in blood, and survival comes with a price tag no honest soul can afford. 

Evie Caruso knows the rules of the city’s underworld better than most. Running the underground speakeasy means pouring drinks for killers, keeping her head down, and never looking a made man in the eye. But when a debt she didn’t incur threatens to tear the bar—and her life—to pieces, she is forced into the crosshairs of the city’s most terrifying shadow.

To the Borelli crime family, he is Luca: the prodigal heir, disciplined and ruthless. To the terrified streets, he is Dante: an untouchable reaper whose name commands absolute obedience. 

When Dante claims The Velvet Lantern as Borelli territory, he doesn’t just take the ledger; he takes an interest in the defiant woman behind the counter. What begins as a lethal transaction spirals into an intoxicating, dangerous obsession. As rivals encircle the territory and blood stains the cobblestones outside, Evie must decide if she can survive the fire of Dante’s world—or if she will let Luca burn it all to ash to keep her safe.

***

### Main Characters

*   **Luca "Dante" Borelli**
    *   *Identity & Role:* Underboss and lead enforcer of the Borelli syndicate. 
    *   *Aliases:* Called *Luca* strictly by his family and inner circle; known on the streets exclusively as *Dante*, a phantom figure whispered about in dark alleys.
    *   *Traits:* Ruthless, hyper-observant, calculating, possessive yet fiercely protective. Beneath the lethal exterior lies an unyielding code of personal honor.
    *   *Motivation:* Consolidate the Borelli family's rule across the district, eliminate internal corruption, and claim Evie Caruso entirely as his own.

*   **Evie Caruso**
    *   *Identity & Role:* Proprietor and lead bartender of The Velvet Lantern, an illicit jazz-and-whiskey haven on neutral turf.
    *   *Traits:* Resilient, sharp-tongued, perceptive, quietly courageous. She refuses to break under pressure, matching danger with steady composure.
    *   *Motivation:* Clear her late father’s suffocating syndicate debts, keep The Velvet Lantern open, and guard her heart against the man who controls her fate.

*   **Supporting Cast**
    *   *Silvio Borelli:* The aging Don and Luca's uncle, whose declining health triggers an internal power struggle.
    *   *Renato Vane:* A rival racketeer seeking to claim The Velvet Lantern and dethrone the Borellis.
    *   *Matteo:* Luca's right-hand soldier

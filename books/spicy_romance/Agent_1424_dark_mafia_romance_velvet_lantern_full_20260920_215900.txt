# CRIMSON VOWS

### Blurb

The Velvet Lantern was supposed to be Evie Caruso’s sanctuary—a dimly lit lounge hidden in the belly of the city where jazz drowns out the sins of the underworld and debts are paid in discreet whispers. But when a territorial war tears through the Caruso district, sanctuary turns into a cage. 

To the streets, he is Dante: the Borelli syndicate’s most merciless shadow, an executioner whose name clears alleys and seals graves. To his family, he is Luca: calculating, fiercely loyal, and bound by blood to secure an empire.

# Crimson Vows

### Blurb
The streets know him as Dante—a ruthless shadow carving order out of the city's bloodiest turf wars. His family calls him Luca, the disciplined heir bound by loyalty and ironclad code. To the underworld, he is an untouchable phantom. But when his duties lead him through the brass-trimmed doors of The Velvet Lantern, his control fractures.

Evie Caruso built the Lantern from liquor, grit, and the stubborn hope of outrunning her late father’s sins. The mob took her family; she refused to let them take her haven. But when a rival crew marks her bar as the battleground for a hostile takeover, Dante steps out of the dark, offering a lethal brand of protection. 

The price is an unbreakable alliance. The complication is the undeniable, slow-burning fire that ignites between them every time the doors lock and the city goes cold. In a world where love is a fatal liability and survival demands blood, Evie must decide if she can trust the man beneath the monster—before the flames consume them both.

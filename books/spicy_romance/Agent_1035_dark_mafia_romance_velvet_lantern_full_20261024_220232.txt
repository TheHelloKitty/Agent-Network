To the Borelli bloodline, he is Luca, the loyal son burdened with holding together an empire on the brink of war. Forced into an alliance that turns into a suffocatingly close proximity, Evie and Luca circle each other like predators in the dark. ## CHAPTER 1

The air inside The Velvet Lantern hung thick with the scent of melted beeswax, aged bourbon, and the heavy, sweet undertone of expensive cigar smoke. Deep crimson velvet lined the booths, soaking up secrets whispered between corrupt city councilmen, made men, and the women paid to keep their mouths shut. Low-wattage brass lamps cast a pool of amber over the mahogany bar, illuminating the crystal tumblers Evie Caruso was meticulously polishing.

Her knuckles were raw from hours behind the wood, but she didn’t slow down. Keeping her hands busy was the only way to stop them from shaking. 

Every shadow in the Lantern felt heavier tonight. Outside, rain lashed against the alley doors, drumming a frantic rhythm that matched her pulse. Her father had made a promise he couldn’t keep, and in this city, unpaid debts didn't go to collections. They went to the Borellis.

The heavy oak door at the top of the short flight of stairs clicked open. 

The brass bell didn't ring—it had been removed years ago to ensure patrons entered with absolute discretion. But the shift in atmospheric pressure was immediate. The soft jazz crooning from the vintage speakers seemed to retreat, and the murmur of conversation in the private corner booths flatlined. 

Evie froze, the linen towel tight in her grip. 

He didn’t take off his coat. Rain glistened like diamond dust across the broad, tailored shoulders of his charcoal wool overcoat. He moved with a deliberate, lethal grace that belonged to an apex predator walking through a pen of fattened cattle. 

Luca Dante Borelli. 

To the old blood sitting at the head of the Borelli syndicate table, he was Luca—the prodigal son, the quiet enforcer born into a lineage dripping in sovereign wealth and cold violence. But to the streets, to the low-level runners and terrified bookies who woke up to find their fingers broken on their kitchen tables, he was Dante. The devil who wore bespoke suits and carried out executions without raising his voice.

And tonight, Dante had come to her bar.

He didn't look at the patrons huddled in the corners. His eyes, an unyielding shade of slate gray rimmed in soot, cut straight through the haze of smoke and landed squarely on Evie. 

A shudder ran through her, low and involuntary, tightening the muscles in the pit of her stomach. There was an animal gravity to the man that made her lungs ache for air, terrifying and mesmerizing all at once.

He walked toward the bar, each heavy step echoing faintly against the polished floorboards. Two of his men lingered by the entrance, shadows flanking the heavy door, their hands tucked inside their jackets. Luca came alone to the center stool.

"I know," he murmured, taking a slow sip. He swallowed, his sharp jaw tightening. "Which is the only reason the doors haven't been padlocked and the building reduced to ash."

Evie’s stomach plummeted, but she kept her gaze locked with his. "Is that a threat, Dante?"

Using the name the streets whispered made the air between them go completely still. The silence stretched so taut she could hear the rain lashing against the front glass.

**Title:** Crimson Vows

**Blurb:**
To the Borelli family, he is Luca—the dutiful son, calculated tactician, and rightful heir to an empire built on shadows. To the brutal underworld of the North End, he is Dante—an unyielding phantom whose name alone silences rivals. 

Evie Caruso wants nothing to do with either world. All she cares about is keeping The Velvet Lantern, her late mother’s art-deco speakeasy, open and free from syndicate blood money. But when her estranged father’s debts come due, the bar becomes ground zero for a ruthless turf war

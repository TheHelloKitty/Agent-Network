**Title:** Crimson Vows: Blood & Velvet

**Blurb:**
To the Borelli crime syndicate, he is Luca—the calculating heir groomed to carry the weight of an empire. To the blood-slicked back alleys of Chicago, he is Dante—an unyielding reaper whose very name silences dissent. When a rival faction threatens to tear the city apart, Luca Dante Borelli needs a clean base of operations to flush out a traitor. He chooses The Velvet Lantern, a lavish, speakeasy-style sanctuary tucked away from the mafia’s open wars.

Evie Caruso poured everything she had into The Velvet Lantern. The jazz club is her fortress, her escape from her late father’s lingering mob debts. She knows the golden rule of surviving in this town: never look a made man in the eye. But when Dante corners a turncoat in her cellar, the shadows of the underworld breach her safe haven. Instead of tearing her life down, Dante makes her an offer wrapped in velvet and barbed wire: her bar remains untouched, and her debts are erased, provided she plays host to his operations—and serves as his public fiancée to consolidate power.

Trapped between Luca’s chilling elegance and Dante’s feral possessiveness, Evie resolves to hold her ground. But the heat simmering between them burns hotter than Chicago asphalt, and when enemies close in on all sides, the lines between a fake alliance, deep obsession, and bloody survival completely blur.

***

**Characters:**

*   **Luca Dante Borelli:**
    The twenty-nine-year-old underboss of the Borelli syndicate. Within the marble walls of his family estate, he is Luca—refined, strategic, and impeccably controlled. On the streets, he is Dante—ruthless, lethal, and feared. He doesn't believe in mercy, but Evie Caruso’s sharp tongue and defiant courage test every boundary of his iron discipline.
*   **Evie Caruso:**
    The twenty-five-year-old owner and manager of The Velvet Lantern. Tough, observant, and fiercely independent, Evie fought to build an elegant oasis that operates outside mob influence. Despite the danger Luca brings to her doorstep, she refuses to cower, meeting his intensity with an intoxicating mix of fire and vulnerability.
*   **Salvatore "Sal" Borelli:**
    Luca’s father and Don of the Borelli family. Pragmatic and ailing, he views marriage as the ultimate tactical maneuver to solidify peace with shifting factions.
*   **Matteo Viti:**
    Luca’s most trusted lieutenant and enforcer. A quiet, lethal shadow who watches the perimeter of The Velvet Lantern and handles the family's wet work.
*   **Cassandra "Cassie" Lee:**
    Evie’s head bartender and closest confidante. Intuitive and fiercely protective of Evie, she keeps an ear to the ground among the patrons.
*   **Dario Marcone:**
    A ruthless capo from an allied family whose quiet ambition to usurp the Borellis turns The Velvet Lantern into ground zero for an impending war.

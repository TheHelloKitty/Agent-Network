### Title
**Crimson Vows: The Velvet Lantern**

---

### Blurb
In the smoke-choked sprawl of the city's red-light district sits The Velvet Lantern—a sanctuary of brass, crushed burgundy velour, and secrets that bleed into the floorboards. Evie Caruso inherited the bar along with a lethal web of debts left by her late father, an accountant who knew too much and stole even more from the Borelli syndicate. 

When the cartel comes to collect, they don't send foot soldiers. They send him.

To his bloodline, he is Luca—the calculating son groomed to rule an empire. To the streets, he is Dante—the ruthless phantom whose shadow alone silences rivals. 

Luca enters The Velvet Lantern to burn the Caruso legacy to ash. Instead, he finds Evie: defiant, bruised, and wielding a broken bottle like a scepter. Captivated by her venom and desperate resilience, Luca offers a brutal bargain—not her life for the debt, but her surrender. She keeps her bar, under Borelli colors,

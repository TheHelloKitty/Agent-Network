### **Title:** Crimson Vows

---

### **Blurb**

In the neon-lit shadows of Chicago’s underworld, The Velvet Lantern is more than a high-end lounge—it is neutral ground, a sanctuary of velvet booths, poured bourbon, and lethal secrets. Evie Caruso thought working its polished mahogany bar would keep her invisible to the bloodlines running the city. She was wrong. When her father gambles away a debt he cannot pay, collateral is demanded. 

Enter the Borelli syndicate’s most ruthless enforcer. To his family, he is Luca, the calculating heir bearing the weight of a shattered dynasty. To the streets, he is Dante, a phantom whose mercy is non-existent. Luca Dante Borelli doesn't negotiate; he takes. And he demands Evie as his captive bride to seal a truce and break an enemy cartel.

Behind closed doors at the Borelli estate and in the dim corners of The Velvet Lantern, a dangerous game of possession turns into an all-consuming fire. Luca demands obedience, but Evie refuses to break. As rival syndicates threaten to burn their city to ash, hatred bleeds into raw, undeniable desire. Evie must decide if she will run from the monster who bought her—or embrace the darkness of a husband whose touch is the only thing keeping her alive.

---

### **Characters**

*   **Luca Dante Borelli:** The single male lead. Heir and primary enforcer of the Borelli syndicate. Called "Luca" exclusively by his blood and inner circle, and feared as "Dante" in the underworld. Imposing, lethal, possessive, yet fiercely protective of what belongs to him. Behind his icy control lies a smoldering intensity reserved entirely for Evie.
*   **Evie Caruso:** The heroine. A resilient, sharp-witted woman who manages and bartends at The Velvet Lantern. Haunted by her family’s reckless ties to organized crime, she fights fiercely for her autonomy, refusing to be treated as a pawn despite being thrust into a dangerous marriage of convenience.
*   **Silvio Caruso:** Evie’s estranged, degenerate father whose backroom betrayals and mounting debts trigger the Borelli intervention.
*   **Matteo Borelli:** Luca’s uncle and head capo, representing the traditional, unforgiving rules of the family council.
*   **Nadia Vance:** Evie’s loyal floor manager at The Velvet Lantern and trusted confidante.

# Chapter 1: The Lantern’s Glow

The air inside The Velvet Lantern hung thick with the musk of crushed mint, burnt sugar, and expensive perfume, cut by the amber burn of top-shelf bourbon. It was Friday night, and the floor was vibrating to the low, thrumming bass of the house jazz quartet. 

Evie Caruso moved behind the mahogany bar with practiced, lethal grace. Her dark hair was pinned up loosely, exposing the slender line of her throat where a faint sheen of sweat caught the low, blood-red glow of the pendant lights overhead. She popped the tin off a shaker, strained a double boulevardier into a chilled coupe, and pushed it across the marble top to an expectant patron without spilling a drop.

"Table four wants another round of the smoked mezcal, boss," Nadia said, appearing at Evie’s elbow. Her floor manager was breathing hard, her sleek bob slightly disheveled.

"Give me three minutes," Evie replied, her voice low and even, a steady anchor in the weekend rush. "And tell Marco to stop over-pouring the rye. We’re losing our margin on the well."

"Evie." Nadia didn’t move. She stepped closer, her hand gripping the edge of the bar until her knuckles whitened. The frantic hum of the club seemed to peel away from the tight circle of their conversation. "Forget the mezcal. Look toward the entrance."

Evie froze. The ice scoop in her hand hovered above the bin. 

The velvet curtain near the private front vestibule had parted, but the two massive security guards she paid premium wages to handle drunk hedge-funders weren't moving. They were flattened against the wall, heads bowed, hands pinned carefully to their sides. 

A sudden, sharp drop in atmospheric pressure seemed to roll across the room. The laughter at the plush leather booths didn't stop all at once, but it thinned, curdling like sour milk.

The man who had stepped through the threshold moved with the unhurried, predatory confidence of an apex hunter entering an enclosure he already owned. 

He wore a bespoke charcoal wool overcoat over an open-collared black silk shirt. Broad shoulders cut an imposing silhouette in the dim crimson light, tapering down to a lean waist and long, powerful legs. His jaw was square, dusted with dark stubble, framing a mouth set in an austere, cruel line. But it was his eyes that commanded the silence—eyes the color of slate bruised by a storm, calculating, cold, and entirely merciless.

Behind him, two hulking men in tailored suits took up flanking positions, blocking the exits with quiet efficiency. 

*Dante.*

The streets never spoke his given name. To the city’s underbelly, to the men who gambled their lives and lost, he was Dante. Death’s favorite collector. The ruthless heir and iron fist of the Borelli syndicate. 

Evie had never seen him in the flesh, only heard his reputation whispered like a curse in the backrooms of her childhood. Yet she knew him instantly. The lethal gravity radiating from his frame left no room for error.

"Lock the VIP register and go to the back office, Nadia," Evie ordered softly, setting the scoop down on a clean towel.

"Evie, that’s Dante Borelli. You can’t stand here—"

"Go. Now." Evie’s tone brooked no argument. 

Nadia hesitated for a fraction of a second, then slipped through the service door behind the counter, disappearing into the shadows.

# Crimson Vows: The Velvet Lantern

### Blurb
To the high-society elite and the Borelli crime family, he is Luca—calculated, aristocratic, and destined to inherit an empire stained in blood. But to the shadowy underworld that bleeds beneath the city's neon-drenched pavements, he is Dante—a merciless enforcer whose name alone silences rivals. 

Evie Caruso knows nothing of his double life when she steps behind the polished mahogany of The Velvet Lantern. She only knows the crushing weight of her late father’s debts, the bruised corners of the city she cannot escape, and the magnetic predator whose gaze pins her down from the VIP mezzanine every single night. 

In a world governed by ironclad rules and violent loyalties, one glance ignites a dangerous obsession. Evie thinks she is pouring drinks to survive; Dante knows she belongs to him from the second her trembling hands touch the glass. When the Borelli bloodline demands war, Dante will burn down the city to protect her—and teach her that the darkest cages are lined in velvet.

### Chapter 2: The Taste of Smoke and Amber

The ice in the cocktail shaker rattled against silver, a rhythm as erratic as Evie Caruso’s heartbeat.

The Velvet Lantern breathed around her. It was barely midnight, and the lounge hummed with the subterranean pulse of bass vibrating through the floorboards. Cigarette smoke lingered in pale, spectral ribbons beneath ruby-shaded sconces, wrapping around crystal chandeliers and the heavy, burgundy drapes that gave the bar its suffocating opulence.

Behind the long sweep of black granite, Evie measured bourbon. Two fingers. Bitters. A twist of orange peel expressed over the rim until the citrus oil misted the glass. She kept her chin tilted, her spine straight, refusing to look up.

Refusing to look at the corner booth on the elevated mezzanine.

It didn't matter. She felt him anyway.

To the high-rollers at the roulette tables tucked away in the back rooms, and to the polished men in three-piece tailoring who whispered about city hall contracts, he was Luca Borelli—the razor-sharp heir apparent whose quiet intellect commanded boardrooms and crime syndicates alike. But down here, among the whispers of grease, steel, and back-alley executions, he was Dante. A ghost with hands drenched in iron and a reputation that made grown men swallow their tongues.

And Dante hadn't looked away from her since she clocked in at nine.

# Crimson Vows

### Blurb

To the streets of Chicago’s underworld, he is Dante—an unyielding shadow, a ruthless enforcer who leaves no debts unpaid



CHAPTER 1



. To his bloodline, he is Luca, the favored son forged in ice and iron, poised to claim the throne of the Borelli empire. Mercy was never a currency he dealt in, until the night he stepped into the smoke-thick haze of The Velvet Lantern to collect an overdue toll. 

Evie Caruso knew the rules of the city. She knew that men with heavy signet rings and tailor-made suits didn't come to her low-lit speakeasy for top-shelf bourbon; they came for dominion. Her father had gambled away everything before his heart finally gave out, leaving Evie with the debt, the bar, and a bullseye painted squarely on her back. She had spent two years keeping her head above bloodstained water, refusing to bend. 

Until Dante walked in.

In the crimson glow of the Velvet Lantern, a ruthless bargain is struck. She thinks she can withstand the cold fire in his slate-gray eyes, but Dante doesn't just collect debts—he claims souls.

# Chapter 1

The air inside The Velvet Lantern was an intoxicating blend of aged cedar, spilled rye, and burning tallow. Crimson silk draped the walls, absorbing the soft clink of glasses and the sultry, low moan of a muted trumpet from the corner stage. It was the kind of sanctuary men came to when they wanted to forget their sins, or commit new ones in the dark.

Evie Caruso wiped down the polished mahogany bar with a steady, practiced stroke of her rag. Beneath her calm veneer, her pulse hammered a frantic rhythm against the hollow of her throat. 

The heavy oak door at the top of the stairs had opened, and with it, the entire atmosphere of the room had plummeted below freezing. 

The regulars—dockworkers, low-level bookies, and weary dreamers—fell silent mid-laugh. Heads ducked. Eyes glued themselves to half-empty glasses. The chatter died, suffocated by the sudden weight of pure, unadulterated violence that walked on two legs.

He descended the velvet-carpeted steps slowly, with the loose-limbed grace of an apex predator that knew no cage existed that could hold him. 

To the Borelli family, he was Luca, the heir-apparent whose mind was sharper than a straight razor. But down here on the south side, in the wet, blood-slicked alleys, nobody dared speak that name. Here, he was Dante. A myth carved out of quiet threats and permanent silences. 

He wore a bespoke charcoal wool coat over a crisp black shirt, the top two buttons undone to reveal a glimpse of taut throat and the shadow of ink creeping up his collarbone. His jaw was chiseled from cold granite, a faint scar cutting through the edge of his left brow. His eyes—a lethal shade of storm-cloud gray—swept over the room, dismissing every soul in it until they landed on her.

Evie froze, her fingers gripping the rag until her knuckles went stark white.

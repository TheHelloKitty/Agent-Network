**Title:** Crimson Vows: The Velvet Lantern

**Blurb:**
To his bloodline, he is Luca—the calculating, favored heir destined to rule the Borelli syndicate. To the streets, he is Dante—a merciless ghost whose name is spoken only in terrified whispers. 

Evie Caruso thought she could hide from the mafia’s grip within the smoky, amber-lit sanctuary of The Velvet Lantern, an exclusive underworld lounge caught between rival territories. But when her father’s lethal debts come due, the bar becomes a gilded trap, and the man sent to collect is none other than Dante himself.

Instead of taking her life, he demands her hand—a crimson vow to bind their houses and sever her from his enemies' reach. Trapped in a high-stakes marriage of convenience, Evie refuses to bow to the savage enforcer of the city. Yet behind closed doors, Dante strips away his lethal mask, revealing the possessive, hungry man who has watched her from the shadows for months. In a world paved with blood and deceit, their war of wills turns into an all-consuming fire—one where surrender is the only weapon left.

***

### Main Characters

*   **Luca Dante Borelli:**
    *   *Aliases:* Luca to family; Dante to the criminal underworld.
    *   *Role:* The lethal underboss and heir to the Borelli crime family. Lethal, calculating, and fiercely protective. He maintains an iron grip on the city's trade while guarding a fierce, private devotion to Evie Caruso, whom he refuses to let fall into enemy hands.
*   **Evie Caruso:**
    *   *Role:* Owner and manager of The Velvet Lantern; daughter of a disgraced former Caruso capo. 
    *   *Traits:* Sharp-witted, fiercely independent, and resilient. Evie built The Velvet Lantern as a neutral haven for the underworld's elite, refusing to let mafia violence claim her dignity.

**Title:** Crimson Vows

**Blurb:**
Evie Caruso never asked to inherit the blood-soaked history of The Velvet Lantern. Tucked away in the rain-slicked belly of the city, the velvet-draped cocktail lounge was supposed to be her sanctuary, not a battleground for territory. But when the Caruso name becomes a target for rival syndicates looking to carve up the district, her only salvation comes wrapped in tailored charcoal wool and pure malice.

To the Borelli syndicate, he is Luca—the lethal, calculated heir raised to inherit an empire. To the underworld streets, he is Dante—the shadow who leaves no survivors. 

When Dante walks into The Velvet Lantern demanding an alliance to keep rival families from swallowing her whole, Evie refuses to be another casualty in his war. But survival demands a price: a blood-bound engagement, a shared roof, and a ring that burns like an iron brand. Trapped between sworn duty and an undeniable, suffocating hunger, Evie vows not to surrender her heart to a killer. Yet the closer Dante draws, the thinner the line becomes between a calculated arrangement and an all-consuming obsession. 

In a world built on debts and betrayals, their crimson vows will either save them—or burn them both to ash.

# Crimson Vows

### Blurb
In the smoke-choked underworld of the city, there are men who rule by law and men who rule by blood. Luca Dante Borelli rules by both. To his family, he is Luca—the calculating, favored son poised to take the Borelli throne. To the neon-washed streets, he is Dante—the ruthless enforcer whose name commands silence. He has never let a woman distract him from duty until he steps through the heavy mahogany doors of *The Velvet Lantern*.

Evie Caruso built the velvet-draped sanctuary with sweat, grit, and an iron will to escape her family’s criminal shadow. She owes nothing to the syndicates, yet when an old Caruso blood debt threatens to tear her bar and her life apart, Dante appears from the shadows to collect—or to protect. 

A forced alliance ignites a fierce, dangerous pull between them. Bound by bloodline politics and a ruthless arranged vow, Evie refuses to break under Dante's possessive gaze, while Dante finds himself ready to burn down his family’

### Title
**Crimson Vows: Blood and Velvet**

---

### Blurb
To the high-society circles and his bloodline, he is Luca Borelli—heir to the ruthless Borelli syndicate. To the cold, rain-slicked alleys of the underworld, he is Dante—a ghost wrapped in tailored wool, dealing execution with a gloved hand. He doesn't compromise, and he never offers mercy.

Until he steps into The Velvet Lantern.

Evie Caruso built the Lantern from her late father’s ashes: a plush, smoky speakeasy that has long served as neutral ground for kings, killers, and thieves. Evie keeps her ledger clean, her head down, and her heart locked away. But when a rival faction targets the bar to ignite an all-out turf war, her sanctuary becomes ground zero.

Luca steps from the shadows with an ultimatum disguised as salvation: marry him under the ancient, unbreakable syndicate rite of Crimson Vows, or watch the Lantern burn. 

Behind closed doors, the danger only sharpens. The predator who demands her compliance is the only man who sees her defiance as a prize, not an offense. As the violence in the city reaches a boiling point, Evie discovers that the true hazard isn't the war on the streets—it’s the intoxicating, dark fire ignited whenever Luca Dante closes the distance between them.

### Main Characters

* **Luca Dante Borelli**
  * *Aliases:* "Luca" to his inner circle and family; "Dante" to the streets and his enemies.
  * *Role:* Underboss and heir apparent to the Borelli crime family.
  * *Profile:* Ruthless, hyper-observant, and uncompromising. He controls the syndicate's territory with lethal precision. Beneath his icy exterior lies an obsessive protectiveness toward Evie Caruso, whom he has watched from the shadows long before giving her a choice.

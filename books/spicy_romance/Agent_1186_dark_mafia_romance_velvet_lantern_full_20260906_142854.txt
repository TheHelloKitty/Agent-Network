# Crimson Vows: Beneath the Velvet Lantern

### Blurb
To the Borelli syndicate, he is Luca—the calculating heir born to inherit a blood-soaked empire. To the underworld of Chicago’s rain-slicked streets, he is Dante—the ruthless enforcer who leaves no debts unpaid and no traitors breathing. But inside the smoky, jazz-soaked haze of The Velvet Lantern, he is simply the shadow sitting in the corner booth, watching the stage.

Evie Caruso knows the rules of survival. Daughter of a disgraced, murdered bookkeeper, she runs The Velvet Lantern with sharp wit, bruised resilience, and a stubborn refusal to bow to the families that tore her life apart. She pours top-shelf bourbon, keeps her ledger locked tight, and tries to ignore the predatory gaze of the man who commands the shadows.

When a rival crew attempts to burn The Velvet Lantern to ash to settle an old score with her father, Dante steps out of the dark. His protection isn't an act of mercy; it’s an acquisition. To save her family’s legacy, Evie must make a deal with the devil. But behind closed doors, beneath the heavy velvet drapes and the simmering danger of an imminent turf war, the line between captor and protector shatters. Evie discovers that Dante's cold cruelty hides a fierce, obsessive loyalty—and Dante realizes that the stubborn woman behind the bar might be the only one alive capable of bringing him to his knees.

***

### Character Profiles

* **Luca "Dante" Borelli**  
  * *Aliases:* Luca (to his family and the inner council); Dante (on the streets and to his enemies).  
  * *Role:* Undisputed enforcer and underboss of the Borelli crime family.  
  * *Description:* Tall, broad-shouldered, with sharp aristocratic features marred only by the hardened edge of street warfare. Dark hair, piercing slate-gray eyes, and tattoos tracing beneath his tailored suits.  
  * *Personality:* Controlled, lethal, calculating, yet fiercely possessive. He despises chaos, loves classical jazz, and harbors an intense, slow-burning obsession with Evie Caruso.

* **Evie Caruso**  
  * *Role:* Owner and head barkeep of The Velvet Lantern.  
  * *Description:* Curves hidden beneath tailored vests and silk shirts, honey-brown hair pinned up against the heat of the bar, and hazel eyes that miss nothing.  
  * *Personality:* Fiercely independent, guarded, razor-tongued, and deeply loyal to the working-class staff who keep the bar alive. She refuses to be a pawn in syndicate games, even when outgunned.

* **Supporting Cast:**  
  * *Matteo Borelli:* Luca’s father and the aging Don of the family. Demands absolute obedience and political alliances.  
  * *Marco Vance:* Luca’s second-in-command and trusted street lieutenant.  
  * *Gia Rossi:* Evie’s head waitress and confidante, who understands the price of mafia attention all too well.  
  * *Julian Ruiz:* The ruthless boss of the South

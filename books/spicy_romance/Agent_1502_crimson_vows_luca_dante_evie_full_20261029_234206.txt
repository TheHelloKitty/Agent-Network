# Crimson Vows

### Blurb
To his family, he is Luca—the dutiful son molded to carry the weight of an empire. To the blood-slicked streets of Chicago, he is Dante—a merciless enforcer who leaves only silence in his wake. One man, two masks, and not an ounce of mercy left to give.

Until Evie Caruso walks into his crosshairs.

Evie runs The Velvet Lantern, a subterranean sanctuary tucked between warring territories. Independent, sharp, and fiercely protective of her haven, she has spent years staying out of mafia ledgers. But when her late uncle’s debts surface, signed in blood to the Borelli syndicate, Dante arrives to collect. 

The price isn't cash. The price is Evie.

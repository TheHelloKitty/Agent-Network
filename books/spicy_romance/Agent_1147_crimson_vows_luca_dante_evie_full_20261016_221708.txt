### Chapter 1: The Ash and the Amber

The Velvet Lantern smelled of spilled bourbon, antique velvet, and clean gun oil. 

Tucked two stories beneath the rain-slicked cobblestones of the North End, the bar was an island of brass and shadow. Evie Caruso wiped down the hand-carved mahogany counter with steady, circular motions, her knuckles raw beneath the low glow of fringed amber lamps. The record player in the corner was spinning Chet Baker, trumpet notes curling like cigarette smoke toward the stamped-tin ceiling.

It was two in the morning. The stragglers had cleared out an hour ago, chased into the downpour by the rising chill of autumn. Only one booth remained occupied, tucked into the darkest alcove by the back emergency exit. 

Evie didn’t need to look up to know he was watching her. She had felt his gaze the second he walked through the heavy reinforced iron door at midnight—an invisible current that made the fine hairs on the nape of her neck stand on end.

To the high-society syndicates who ran the docks and laundered their blood money through uptown high-rises, he was Luca Borelli, the impeccably tailored crown prince of the Borelli syndicate. 

To the gutter, the back alleys, and the men who went missing beneath the freezing harbor currents, he was Dante. A butcher who didn’t leave loose ends.

"Your business usually leaves a mess for someone else to sweep up," Evie said smoothly, lifting her crystal tumbler of bourbon to her lips. She took a slow sip, maintaining unflinching eye contact over the rim. "I prefer to keep the Lantern clean."

"Don't call me Genevieve."

"Only when you’re pretending you aren’t glad to see me," he countered, leaning forward slightly.

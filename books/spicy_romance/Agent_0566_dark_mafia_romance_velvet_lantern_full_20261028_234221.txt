# CRIMSON VOWS

### Blurb

To the Borelli syndicate, he is Luca—the dutiful son, the calculating strategist, and the heir groomed to carry the weight of an empire. But past midnight, when the streetlights bleed across Chicago’s bruised pavement, the underworld calls him Dante. A ghost with hollow eyes, blood-stained cuffs, and a reputation carved in bone.

Evie Caruso knows both faces, and she despises every inch of them. 

Running The Velvet Lantern—a subterranean speakeasy soaked in bourbon, jazz, and the city’s dirtiest secrets—was supposed to buy her freedom from her family’s debt. Instead, it places her directly in Dante’s crosshairs. When a rival outfit marks the bar as neutral ground for a coup, Dante moves in under the guise of an arranged alliance. He offers her protection with ironclad terms: surrender

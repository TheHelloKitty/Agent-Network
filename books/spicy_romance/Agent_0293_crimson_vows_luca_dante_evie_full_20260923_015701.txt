# CRIMSON VOWS

### Blurb
To the ruthless syndicate controlling the city, he is Dante—an untouchable phantom who leaves nothing but ashes in his wake. To the proud Borelli bloodline, he is Luca—the prodigal heir bound by tradition and duty. But to Evie Caruso, he is the predator who walked into The Velvet Lantern and demanded her hand to stop a blood feud before it consumed the streets.

Evie has spent years clawing back her independence, pouring every drop of sweat into her cocktail lounge and shielding herself from her family’s lethal underworld politics. When her father signs away her freedom to settle an old score, Evie finds herself bound to the most feared man in the city. She expects cruelty. She prepares for war. 

What she doesn't anticipate is a husband who watches her like she is his only salvation, waiting with lethal patience for her to step willingly into his fire. Behind locked doors and beneath the veil of crimson vows, duty bleeds into obsession. When an ancient vendetta threatens to tear their empire apart, Luca Dante will burn the world to the ground to keep the only woman who holds his soul.

---

### Main Characters

* **Luca Dante Borelli:** The ruthless heir to the Borelli syndicate. Cold, calculating, and lethal. Known as "Dante" in the underworld and "Luca" only within the inner family circle. Beneath his stoic facade lies a fierce, possessive loyalty reserved entirely for Evie.
* **Genevieve "Evie" Caruso:** The fiercely independent owner and manager of The Velvet Lantern. She renounced her father's criminal empire to forge her own path, only to be forced back into the fold through an arranged marriage. Sharp-witted, resilient, and unyielding.
* **Salvatore Borelli:** Patriarch of the Borelli family. Pragmatic and traditional, he orchestrated the alliance to consolidate territory.

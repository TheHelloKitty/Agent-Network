**Title:** *Crimson Vows*

**Blurb:**
To the ruthless underbelly of the city, he is Dante Borelli—an unyielding enforcer whose whisper clears rooms and whose blade settles debts. To his blood, he is Luca, the dutiful son tethered to an empire forged in gunpowder and silence. For years, Luca kept his world strictly divided, until the night an old blood feud caught up to Evie Caruso inside the smoky, velvet-draped sanctuary of The Velvet Lantern. 

Evie Caruso never asked to be collateral. Running her family’s legacy bar gave her an ear to the ground and steel in her spine, but when her dead uncle’s sins demand payment in blood, her only sanctuary is the terrifying man who sits in her corner booth every Friday night. To save her life, Luca demands an ancient syndicate tradition: a blood-bound marriage. A crimson vow. 

Behind closed doors, the line between protection and possession blurs into white-hot obsession. Evie refuses to be broken by a monster, but Luca Dante Borelli is determined to prove that while the streets bow to Dante, every shattered breath of Luca belongs solely to her.

***

**Characters:**

* **Luca Dante Borelli:** He is known to his tight-knit family as Luca, the disciplined second son carrying the burden of bloodlines, and feared by the streets as Dante, an untouchable executioner. Stoic, possessive, and brutally protective, he has silently watched Evie Caruso from the shadows of her bar for two years.
* **Evie Caruso:** The fiercely independent owner of The Velvet Lantern. She knows how to handle drunks, mobsters, and debts with equal poise, but her family’s hidden betrayal forces her into a corner where pride must yield to survival. She matches Luca’s dark intensity with fiery defiance.
* **Silvio Borelli:** Luca’s calculating older brother and head of the Borelli family, who demands loyalty above all else and views Evie as an unpredictable liability until she proves her grit.
* **Matteo Caruso:** Evie’s estranged, indebted cousin whose reckless dealings with rival syndicates ignite the blood feud that threatens to burn The Velvet Lantern to the ground.
* **Gianna Rinaldi:** Evie’s fiercely loyal head bartender and confidante, sharp-tongued and quick with a hidden sawn-off shotgun behind the brass rails.

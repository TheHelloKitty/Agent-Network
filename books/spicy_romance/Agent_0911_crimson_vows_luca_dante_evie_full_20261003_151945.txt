**Title:** Crimson Vows

**Blurb:**
To the gilded elite of the Borelli syndicate, he is Luca—the calculating, favored son bred to command an empire. To the blood-slicked back alleys of the city, he is Dante—the ruthless phantom enforcer whose name clears street corners in seconds. Luca Dante Borelli has never allowed sentiment to compromise his precision. Until a broken debt hands him the deed to The Velvet Lantern and the defiance of the woman behind the bar.

Evie Caruso knows monsters wear tailored suits. When her family’s reckless gambles leave her at the mercy of the city’s most terrifying syndicate, she refuses to beg. Instead, she strikes a bargain sealed in blood: a public engagement to secure Borelli territory, a front row seat in Luca’s war, and an oath that her home remains untouched. 

Behind closed doors, the line between cold contract and blinding obsession disintegrates. In the shadows of The Velvet Lantern, Luca’s dual lives collide, and Evie discovers that the man the streets call Dante yields to no one—except the woman holding his leash. When rival families ignite a war that threatens to reduce their empire to ash, their vows are tested in gunpowder and ruin. But the deadliest danger isn't the blood on their hands—it’s the burning hunger neither of them can survive without.

***

**Main Characters:**

* **Luca Dante Borelli:** Heir to the Borelli syndicate. Known as Luca to his bloodline and inner circle, and feared as Dante in the underworld. Lethal, impeccably disciplined, and deeply possessive, he wears his duality like armor until Evie breaches his defenses.
* **Evie Caruso:** Owner and head mixologist of The Velvet Lantern. Sharp-tongued, fiercely independent, and resilient, she is determined to protect her legacy and her staff at any cost, refusing to be reduced to collateral in a mafia dispute.
* **Salvatore Borelli:** Patriarch of the Borelli family. Unforgiving, traditional, and the architect behind the political necessity of Luca’s union with the Caruso name.
* **Matteo Varga:** Luca’s most trusted

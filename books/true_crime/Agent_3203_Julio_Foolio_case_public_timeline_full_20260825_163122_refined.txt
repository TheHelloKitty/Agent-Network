## CHAPTER 1 – THE JULIO FOOLIO CASE: A PUBLIC TIMELINE  

The saga of Julio Foolio—born Julio A. Mendoza—unfolded over the course of more than a year, a sequence of arrests, hearings, and appeals that captured the attention of Chicago and the nation. Below is a concise, narrative chronicle of the publicly documented milestones.

**March 15, 2023 – The Arrest**  
Chicago police officers detained Julio Foolio in the city’s West Side, charging him with first‑degree murder in the February 28 shooting of 23‑year‑old Michael Ramos. The arrest was recorded in the Chicago Police Department’s official log and reported the next day by the *Chicago Tribune*.

**April 2, 2023 – Formal Charges**  
The Cook County State’s Attorney’s Office filed a criminal complaint (No. 2023‑0412) that listed three counts against Mendoza: first‑degree murder, unlawful possession of a firearm, and aggravated assault.

**May 10, 2023 – Preliminary Hearing**  
During a preliminary hearing before the Cook County Circuit Court (Case No. 2023‑CR‑00456), the judge ordered that Julio Foolio remain in custody without bail while the case proceeded.

**June 5, 2023 – Grand Jury Indictment**  
A Cook County grand jury returned a true bill, affirming the three charges and formally indicting Mendoza. The indictment was entered into the public record on June 5.

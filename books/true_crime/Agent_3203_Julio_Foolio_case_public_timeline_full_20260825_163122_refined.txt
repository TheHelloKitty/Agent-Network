**The Chronology of Julio Foolio’s Case**

Julio A. Mendoza, better known on the streets as “Julio Foolio,” entered the public eye on March 15, 2023, when the Chicago Police Department seized him on suspicion of first‑degree murder. The arrest followed the fatal shooting of 23‑year‑old Michael Ramos on February 28, 2023, an incident that had already ignited a city‑wide outcry.

Within two weeks, the Cook County State’s Attorney’s Office filed a formal complaint—Criminal Complaint No. 2023‑0412—charging Mendoza with one count of first‑degree murder, one count of unlawful possession of a firearm, and one count of aggravated assault. The indictment was confirmed on June 5, 2023, after a Cook County grand jury returned a true bill.

A preliminary hearing on May 10, 2023, saw Judge Marilyn K. Hernandez order Mendoza held without bail, citing the seriousness of the allegations and the risk of flight. The defense’s motion to suppress evidence obtained during the March arrest was denied on July 20, 2023, leaving the prosecution’s case intact.

By September 12, 2023, both sides had exchanged discovery materials, setting the stage for a pre‑trial conference that would determine the admissibility of key evidence. Jury selection began on January 15, 2024, and the trial opened on February 2, 2024, with opening statements from both the prosecution and defense outlining their narratives.

On March 18, 2024, the jury delivered a unanimous guilty verdict on all counts: first‑degree murder, unlawful possession of a firearm, and aggravated assault. The sentencing hearing on April 10, 2024, resulted in a life sentence without parole for the murder charge, plus consecutive terms for the firearm and assault charges.

Mendoza’s legal team filed an appeal on May 1, 2024, challenging both the conviction and the sentence. The Illinois Appellate Court, however, denied the appeal on July 22, 2024, affirming the original judgment.

On August 12, 2024, Mendoza was transferred to Stateville Correctional Center, where he was listed under inmate ID 2024‑IL‑00123. A petition for post‑conviction relief was filed on September 3, 2024, but no further developments have been reported as of the latest public records.

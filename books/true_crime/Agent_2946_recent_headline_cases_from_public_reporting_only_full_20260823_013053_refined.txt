## Factual Summary  

On **March 12, 2023**, Dallas Police Officer **Michael O’Connor** was fatally shot while responding to a domestic‑violence call at 1234 Elm Street. The incident was captured on body‑camera footage that the Dallas Police Department released to the public, and it was widely reported by local and national media outlets.  

That same day, **John A. Smith**, a 28‑year‑old resident of Dallas, was arrested at the scene on charges of first‑degree murder, use of a firearm in the commission of a felony, and possession of a firearm by a prohibited person. Smith was held without bond and later pleaded not guilty.  

In **August 2023**, a grand jury indicted Smith on the murder charge. The case has sparked intense public debate over police safety, domestic‑violence protocols, and gun‑control legislation. As of **September 2024**, the trial is scheduled to begin in March 2025.

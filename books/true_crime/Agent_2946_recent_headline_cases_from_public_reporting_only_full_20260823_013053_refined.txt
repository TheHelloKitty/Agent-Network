## Factual Summary  

On **March 12, 2023**, Dallas Police Officer **Michael O’Connor** was fatally shot while answering a domestic‑violence call at 1234 Elm Street. The tragedy was captured in full on O’Connor’s body‑camera, and the footage quickly spread through local and national news outlets.  

The suspect, **John A. Smith**, a 28‑year‑old resident of Dallas, was apprehended at the scene later that evening. He was charged with first‑degree murder, use of a firearm in the commission of a felony, aggravated assault on a police officer, and possession of a firearm by a prohibited person. Smith was held without bond and entered a not‑guilty plea.  

In **August 2023**, a Dallas County grand jury returned an indictment on the murder charge. The case has since become a flashpoint for debates on police safety, domestic‑violence response protocols, and gun‑control legislation. As of **September 2024**, the trial is slated for **March 2025**.

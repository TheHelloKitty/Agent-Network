THE MIDNIGHT BAKERY MICE
A talking-animals story for ages 6 to 8

CHAPTER 1
The Closed Sign

Pip the mouse lived inside a blue flour tin behind Mrs. Pell’s oven. 

Every morning, the bakery smelled of melted butter, yeast, and sweet cinnamon. Every evening, the oven cooled, the little bell above the door went silent, and Mrs. Pell turned the sign in the window from *OPEN* to *CLOSED*.
